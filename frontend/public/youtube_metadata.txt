### 📖 The Problem & The Solution
A sudden storm forecast threatens Scene 42—a crucial outdoor rooftop confrontation on Shibuya Tower with principal talent Emma Carter and Daniel. In traditional Hollywood productions, rescheduling requires hours of chaotic phone calls, union overtime disputes, and tens of thousands in idle crew penalties ($79,800+).

Agentic FilmOps changes everything. In under 4 minutes, a coordinated network of 6 specialized domain agents and standardized MCP tool buses:
1. Detects Doppler radar rain probabilities (92%).
2. Traverses the Production Resource Network to identify cast, location, and equipment dependencies.
3. Automatically negotiates with talent agencies via LLM-powered structured comms.
4. Generates 3 Pareto-optimal replan options evaluated by a deterministic Constraint Solver.
5. Executes automated multi-system re-bookings with one click upon Producer approval.
